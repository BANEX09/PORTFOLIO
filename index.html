About

A bit about me

I'm Michael — a front-end developer who got here by rebuilding the interfaces I admired until I understood exactly why they worked.

That's still how I learn. I picked apart Netflix's signup flow and a startup landing page line by line, then rebuilt both from an empty file. Nothing dragged in, nothing copied — just HTML and CSS until the layout held at every width.

Today I build sites for people who need something real on the internet: a landing page that converts, a school site parents can actually navigate, a portfolio that looks like its owner cares. I'm deep in JavaScript now and heading toward React next.
